# Blinky — Offline Windows 20-20-20 Eye Care Timer

### Eye care, on autopilot.

**Blinky** is a tiny, privacy-first **Windows eye strain reminder** and **20-20-20 break timer**. Every 20 minutes, it dims your screen for 20 seconds and reminds you to look at something 20 feet away—the ophthalmologist-recommended 20-20-20 rule.

Set it once, keep working, and let Blinky help reduce digital eye strain without accounts, subscriptions, telemetry, or distracting notifications.

## What is Blinky?

Blinky is an **offline screen break reminder for Windows 10 and Windows 11**. It automatically enforces regular eye breaks while you work, study, code, or browse. Unlike cloud-based wellness apps, Blinky runs locally on your PC and is designed for people who want a simple, lightweight, private eye-care timer.

## ✨ Features

- **Automatic 20-20-20 reminders** — every 20 minutes, take a 20-second break and look 20 feet away
- **Screen-dimming break overlay** — makes it clear when it is time to rest your eyes
- **100% offline and private** — no accounts, network calls, tracking, or telemetry during normal use
- **Dashboard window** — opens on launch, shows live status and every action
- **Floating quick-access button** — a small draggable icon that lives on your screen; click it anytime to open the dashboard, or hide it entirely
- **System tray app** — right-click for the full menu
- **Pause, resume, or break now** — fully adjustable reminder interval and break duration
- **Optional strict mode** — makes a break impossible to skip for people who want stronger enforcement
- **Multi-monitor support** — the break overlay covers every connected screen
- **Launches with Windows** — enabled by default and easy to turn off
- **Manual update check** — checks only when you click the button, never automatically in the background

## 📥 Download and Install

1. Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `Blinky-Setup.exe`.
2. Run the installer — no administrator rights required.
3. Optionally select **Create a desktop shortcut**.
4. Done. Blinky opens automatically and runs quietly in your system tray.

**Uninstalling** is just as clean: **Settings → Apps → Blinky → Uninstall**, like any normal Windows app.

> Windows SmartScreen may show a “protect your PC” warning the first time you run the installer. This is normal for independently distributed apps that are not code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## 🖥️ System Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- No internet connection required to use the app
- Approximately 30 MB of disk space

## 👀 Why use the 20-20-20 rule?

Long periods of uninterrupted screen time can contribute to tired, dry, or uncomfortable eyes. The 20-20-20 rule is a simple habit: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Blinky automates the reminder so you do not have to watch the clock.

Blinky is a productivity and wellness reminder, not a medical device. If you have persistent eye symptoms, consult an eye-care professional.

## 🔒 Privacy

Blinky makes zero network calls during normal use. The only exception is the **Check for Updates** button—and even that only runs when you click it yourself, fetching a small public version file from this repository. Nothing about you or your device is ever sent anywhere.
